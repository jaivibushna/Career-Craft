Top 20 HR Interview Questions

Prepare confidently for your next interview with these commonly asked HR questions.

1. Tell me about yourself.

Give a brief introduction about your education, skills, projects, and career goals.

2. Why do you want to work here?

Show that you've researched the company and explain why it matches your career goals.

3. What are your strengths?

  • Communication
  • Teamwork
  • Problem Solving
  • Quick Learning

4. What are your weaknesses?

Mention a real weakness and explain how you're improving it.

5. Where do you see yourself in 5 years?

Discuss your professional growth and desire to contribute to the organization.

Interview Tips

  • Dress professionally.
  • Arrive on time.
  • Maintain eye contact.
  • Be confident and honest.
  • Ask thoughtful questions at the end.
